How to explore

Lab 1 — Latch

Press START, observe RUN remain ON, then press STOP to release it.

Lab 2 — Count

Press START. Simulated boxes are counted as they pass the sensor, or press SENSOR PULSE manually. At the preset of 10, the motor stops.

Lab 3 — Timers

Press START to begin STAR. After 10 seconds the demonstration transfers to DELTA. Following 20 additional seconds the timer triggers an overload stop.

Lab 4 — Box counting

Run a conveyor model with a motor indication, running timer, counted boxes and a simulated sensor pulse.
