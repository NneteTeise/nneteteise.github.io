Lab 03: Star–Delta Motor Timer

Timed star-to-delta transition with overload timing and interlocking.

▶ Try interactive simulationDownload original TIA project (.zip)

What the system does

The screenshots identify an S_PULSE star period of 10 seconds, mutual exclusion of star and delta outputs, and an S_ODT overload-related timer of 20 seconds after delta starts.

Functional stages

  1. Start/Stop command maintains the motor run condition
  2. Star output remains active during a 10-second S_PULSE period
  3. Delta output then takes over; an S_ODT 20-second timer demonstrates an overload-related switch state

Technology & evidence
