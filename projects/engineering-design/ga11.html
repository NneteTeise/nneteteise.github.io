Brief and proposed product

The group proposed a low-cost, repairable adjustable analogue PCB for a technical school or workshop. The submitted plan identifies an LM324N operational amplifier, reference Zener diode, transistor output stage, potentiometer adjustment, LED indicator and terminal blocks. The stated objectives covered design, manufacture and testing while controlling cost and documenting project risks.

Reported acceptance checks

The QA table reports pass results for operation from a specified 20 V DC supply, adjustable voltage, LED indication, circuit mounting, identifiable terminals, output measurement, safe operation and enclosure fit.

Evidence distinction: The report records these QA outcomes, but separate instrument readings, a manufacturing log and original oscilloscope files were not provided. The portfolio does not independently verify them.

Project planning evidence

1. Client needs

Affordability, output adjustability, ease of repair and accessible components for small DC projects.

2. Risks and changes

Component availability, footprint mismatch, PCB routing, soldering faults and potential budget overruns were documented with mitigations.

3. Value & evaluation

The report considers maintenance value, replaceable components, estimated cost, QA checks and proposed improvements.

What this demonstrates

Budget estimation, sensitivity to changes in unit costs and quantities, and how engineering decisions affect project economics.

Source note: The report's bill of materials lists a Zener diode with a different part number from the narrative. The planner uses only the stated R4 price and avoids selecting a specific variant.

Recommendations and limitations

The report recommends clearer calibration markings, earlier component sourcing, and potential remote-control capability in a future version. The available materials document the engineering and business evaluation, but do not include a full set of instrument measurements that independently verifies voltage or current performance.
